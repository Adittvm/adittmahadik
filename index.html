Different background. Useful perspective.

I study Finance and Data Science at UNC Charlotte, which means I’m usually thinking about both the human decision and the system behind it.

My path into design came through building: an internal workflow at Truist, an AI finance prototype with AWS, a consulting organization for nonprofits, and data visualizations in research and investing. Across all of them, I kept returning to the same question: how can this be clearer for the person using it?

I’m now bringing that product mindset into interaction design—learning quickly, prototyping ideas, and using feedback to move from an ambiguous problem to something people can understand and use.
